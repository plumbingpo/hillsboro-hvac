Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Hillsboro HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and guests notice the drafts and the stuffy dining room right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Hillsboro

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast, throughout Hillsboro and the surrounding area.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your service records. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
